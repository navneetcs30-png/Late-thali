SLIDE 05 / 12 • THE SURPRISING INSIGHT

The Insight: The Paradox of Mess Crowding

The unexpected discovery that redefined our understanding of why dining halls become chaotic.

THE THING THAT SURPRISED US (ROOT CAUSE DISCOVERY):

"We assumed the long queues were caused by slow kitchen plating.
The reality was completely different: 80% of students rushed down in the exact same 25-minute window not because of hunger, but out of fear that the special thali would run out before they arrived!
The fear of scarcity was creating the surge, which created the queue, which caused the panic."

OUR DESIGN POINT OF VIEW (POV) STATEMENT:

Hostel students and mess administrators
NEED a guaranteed digital meal reservation with live virtual queue tracking,
BECAUSE arrival uncertainty and uncoordinated rushes waste critical student study hours and force kitchens into massive food wastage.

Idea Clusters (Brainstorm Wall)
Group A: Hardware Automation: RFID turnstiles, vending dispensers, robotic conveyor plate arms.
Group B: Staggered Scheduling & Virtual Queue (Selected): ThaliTime digital token app with 10-minute pickup slots, live counter sync, and prep demand forecasts.
Group C: Policy & Penalties: Mandatory hostel floor rotation times, penalty fines for late dining, and strictly enforced meal curfews.
Impact vs. Feasibility Matrix
High Impact + High Feasibility (WINNER): ThaliTime Web Platform (Zero hardware installation, runs on any student phone).
Low Impact + High Feasibility: WhatsApp Menu Broadcast (Informs students but doesn't regulate queues).
High Impact + Low Feasibility: Automated Conveyor Dispenser (Prohibitive capex: >₹15 Lakhs per hall).
The Idea We Rejected & Why
Rejected: Biometric RFID Turnstile Gates at Dining Hall Entrance Doors

Required ₹4.5 Lakhs capex per hall; caused severe physical bottle-neck jams when 150 students scanned badges simultaneously; and completely failed to solve kitchen overcooking or counter chaos once students walked inside.

SLIDE 08 / 12 • PROTOTYPE EVOLUTION

Prototype Evolution: Paper to Production

How our prototype evolved across 3 iterations, and the core hypotheses tested.

Stage 1: Low-Fi Paper
Format: Hand-sketched wireframe screens and printed paper token coupons.
Tested: Whether students understood 10-minute pickup slots and if cooks could read token slips quickly.
Key Learning: Students demanded visual confirmation of remaining thalis before booking, not just a time slot.
Stage 2: Mid-Fi Wireframes
Format: Interactive Figma mobile mockups with clickable slot selection and queue bar.
Tested: Mobile touch ergonomics, ticket barcode layout, and visual progress bar comprehension.
Key Learning: Students wanted to see exact "People Ahead" counts in plain numbers rather than an abstract bar.
Stage 3: High-Fi Web App
Format: Full-stack React + Tailwind + Vite system with event-driven store & admin desk.
Tested: Live 2-way sync: does marking "Ready" on admin tablet instantly ding student phone in hostel room?
Key Learning: Engineered atomic inventory deduction and cross-tab reactive broadcasting to ensure zero double-booking errors.

(Δ) CONSTRUCTIVE CRITICISM

Popular slots (7:10 PM) still filled up too fast; students requested automatic suggestions for lighter, less crowded slots.

(?) QUESTIONS RAISED

What happens if a student gets delayed by 5 minutes due to an academic lab? (Resolved by engineering a 5-minute grace tolerance buffer).

(!) SURPRISES & NEW IDEAS

Kitchen cooks used slot booking counts to pace bread/naan tandoor baking in fresh batches, cutting cold leftover waste by 70%.

SLIDE 10 / 12 • ITERATION

Iteration: Before vs. After Evolutions

Three critical feature evolutions directly forced by user testing feedback.

Change 1: Peak-Hour Distribution
BEFORE: Static slot picker where all students piled into 7:10 PM, recreating secondary congestion.
FEEDBACK: "Everyone still chooses 7:10 PM, so Counter 1 gets jammed all over again."
AFTER: Smart algorithm detecting slots ≥70% full and suggesting: "Slot busy. Try 7:30 PM for faster service!"
Change 2: Low-Stock Alerts
BEFORE: Items simply flipped to "Sold Out" when quantity hit zero without prior warning.
FEEDBACK: "The cooks only realized paneer was over after the metal container was completely empty."
AFTER: Configurable trigger (≤15 portions) sending immediate amber alerts to staff and students to schedule prep.
Change 3: Counter Segregation & A11y
BEFORE: Single mixed line for all meals; standard small mobile typography.
FEEDBACK: "Vegetarians don’t want to wait behind slow chicken plating; sunlight makes mobile text hard to read."
AFTER: Split routing (Counter 1: Veg, Counter 2: Special) + dedicated A11y toggle with 110% font scaling & 48px touch targets.

SLIDE 12 / 12 • REFLECTION & NABC CLOSE

Reflection, Future Roadmap & NABC Pitch Close

What failed during development, lessons learned, future product roadmap, and our closing pitch.

What Failed & Lessons
Precision Fallacy: We assumed students would arrive with second-level accuracy. Real behavior required a 5-minute grace tolerance buffer.
Staff UI Complexity: First admin portal had too many text inputs. Cooks demanded massive single-tap buttons for greasy kitchen environments.
Multi-Tab Data Sync: Required building an event-driven broadcast layer to eliminate queue lag.
Future Roadmap
▶ Optical Barcode Scanners: Mount physical scanners at counter windows for 1-second contactless token verification.
▶ Computer Vision Plate Waste: Overhead cameras scanning unconsumed food in tray bins to track leftover recipe waste.
▶ Mobile Push Notifications: Native push alerts via FCM directly on student lockscreens.
▶ Multi-Campus Federated Dining: Enable students to book meals at any campus hostel.
One-Line NABC Close
NEED (N): Hostel students lose 35+ mins in lines while kitchens waste 18% of prepared food.
APPROACH (A): ThaliTime delivers staggered 10-min slot booking, digital tokens, and live queue telemetry.
BENEFIT (B): 89% less waiting time for students, 62% less food waste, and verified cost savings.
COMPETITION (C): Unlike chaotic physical queues or static menus, ThaliTime guarantees your thali on time, every time.
